# Principles

1. Simplest thing that meets the spec; delete before adding; justify every component and dependency.
2. Prove it on the real thing; "it compiles" and "tests pass" are not proof.
3. Fix the root cause; after two failed fixes, write down your assumption and test it.
4. Never lower a pass condition to get a pass.
5. Pointers, not pasted text; summaries, not raw output.
6. Plain words to people.
