# Principles

1. Simplest thing that meets the spec; delete before adding; justify every component and dependency.
2. Name the data first.
3. Prove it on the real thing; "it compiles" and "tests pass" are not proof.
4. Fix the root cause; after two failed fixes, write down your assumption and test it.
5. Never lower a pass condition to get a pass.
6. Best practice by default: accessible, clear empty, loading and error states, secure, no secrets in code.
7. Small steps, each ending in a check.
8. Pointers, not pasted text; summaries, not raw output.
9. Plain words to people.
