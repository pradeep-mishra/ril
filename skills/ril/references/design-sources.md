# Design sources

Fetch with `curl` or raw markdown, never a summary; record the page's date or commit.

- macOS, iOS: Apple HIG, `<page>.md` under `developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/`.
- Windows: Microsoft Learn, Windows app design.
- Linux: GNOME HIG or KDE HIG.
- Android: developer.android.com design and the Material Components docs.
- Web: WCAG 2.2 and WAI-ARIA Authoring Practices.
- Electron: the host OS's standard, per target OS.
- Any: NN/g ten usability heuristics.
- Other: find its authority, the person confirms it.
